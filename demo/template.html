As-Built Reconciler

The crew's closeout notes say one thing. The GIS says another. This reads the notes, compares every cable and splice with the inventory, and lists what to fix before someone assigns a circuit to fibers that are not there.

2GIS inventory

Drop your GIS inventory here Cables as .geojson and splices as .csv. Drop both at once or one at a time.

File formats

Cables (.geojson): one feature per span, with properties cable_id, fiber_count, from_struct, to_struct, length_ft, placement (underground or aerial).

Splices (.csv): columns closure_id, structure, backbone_cable, bb_start, bb_end, branch_cable, br_start, br_end.

Records (.json): what the extract command writes. Sample files are in the repository's examples folder.

Files are read in your browser and are not stored. Notes are sent to Claude only when you press Extract. Do not load records you are not allowed to share.

A demonstration on a made-up project. The notes, the inventory and every name in them are synthetic. The records shown at first are the answer key I wrote by hand; with Claude available, the page reads the notes live and scores the result against that key. You can also load your own notes, records and inventory. The comparison itself uses fixed rules and no model.
